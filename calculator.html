Builder's Tips

Ordering Cement

  • Always add 5-10% extra for waste and spillage
  • C20 mix (1:2:4) uses ~6 bags per m³
  • C25 mix uses ~7 bags per m³
  • Plaster (15mm) covers ~30m² per 50kg bag

Ordering Blocks

  • ~10 blocks per m² for standard wall
  • Add 5% for breakages during transport
  • 6-inch for internal walls, 8-inch for external
  • ISSB blocks need no mortar between courses

Ordering Roofing

  • Account for 15% overlap between sheets
  • 28-gauge lasts longer in harsh weather
  • Don't forget ridge caps, gutters & flashings
  • Add 2-3 extra sheets for cutting waste

Ordering Paint

  • ~8m² per litre per coat
  • Always buy primer/undercoat first
  • 2 coats standard, 3 for dark colours over light
  • 20L bucket covers ~120m² (2 coats)
